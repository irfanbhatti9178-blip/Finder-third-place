What Is a Third Place?

A third place is somewhere that isn’t home (first place) or work/school (second place). It’s a welcoming public or semi-public space where people can relax, meet others, study, play, or simply spend time. Classic examples include cafés, parks, libraries, community centers, and local hangouts.

How to Find a Third Place Near Me

Use the tool above: tap Use My Location or pick a country and city, choose a category (or leave it on All), then press Find Third Place. Results appear as cards and map markers with directions.

Best Types of Third Places

  • Cafés & coffee shops – great for casual meetups and working
  • Parks & outdoor spaces – free, open, and social
  • Libraries – quiet study and community programs
  • Community centers & youth spaces – activities and local events
  • Coworking spaces – work alongside others
  • Gaming, sports & music venues – shared hobbies and nightlife

Why Third Places Are Useful

Third places reduce isolation, support casual social contact, and give people low-pressure environments to study, work, or unwind. Whether you’re new in town or looking for a change of scenery, a good third place makes everyday life better.
